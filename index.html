CV Writing Principles

1Keep it to two pages maximum. Recruiters spend 6–7 seconds on first screening. Make every word earn its place.
2Lead with achievements, not duties. Replace "Responsible for…" with "Increased X by Y% through Z."
3Customise for every application. Mirror keywords from the job description naturally — never send the same CV twice.
4Avoid tables, columns and images. ATS systems struggle with complex formatting. Clean, linear layouts perform best.
5Open with a professional summary. Three to four lines capturing who you are, what you offer, and where you're headed.
6Proofread relentlessly. A single typo can disqualify you. Read aloud, use spellcheck, and have a second pair of eyes review.

Top Interview Success Tips

  • 1Research the company thoroughly — understand their mission, values, recent news and competitors before the interview.
  • 2Prepare 3-5 powerful stories using the STAR method that demonstrate your key achievements and problem-solving abilities.
  • 3Dress appropriately — research the company culture and dress one level above their everyday attire.
  • 4Arrive 10-15 minutes early — punctuality signals respect and reliability. For virtual interviews, test your tech beforehand.
  • 5Ask thoughtful questions — prepare 3-5 questions about the role, team, culture and growth opportunities.
  • 6Follow up within 24 hours — send a personalized thank-you email referencing specific points from the conversation.
