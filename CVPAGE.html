CV Writing Principles

What recruiters and hiring managers actually look for.

1 Keep it to two pages maximum. Recruiters spend 6–7 seconds on first screening. Make every word earn its place.
2 Lead with achievements, not duties. Replace "Responsible for…" with "Increased X by Y% through Z."
3 Customise for every application. Mirror keywords from the job description naturally — never send the same CV twice.
4 Avoid tables, columns and images. ATS systems struggle with complex formatting. Clean, linear layouts perform best.
5 Open with a professional summary. Three to four lines capturing who you are, what you offer, and where you're headed.
6 Proofread relentlessly. A single typo can disqualify you. Read aloud, use spellcheck, and have a second pair of eyes review.
